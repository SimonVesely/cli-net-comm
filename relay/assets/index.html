cli-net-comm relay TLS 1.3 end-to-end

Meeting point for the cli-net-comm terminal chat. Clients behind NAT connect here over WebSocket, find each other and get paired. The chat itself is encrypted between the two clients, so this server only forwards ciphertext.

    How it works

    1Listen

    The client opens a WebSocket to /listen under its name and appears in the list above.

    2Invite

    Picking someone sends an invite through the relay. They accept with a or decline with d.

    3Chat

    Both sides run a TLS 1.3 handshake through the pipe. Compare the fingerprint shown in both terminals.
